# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

This project is maintained by one person in their spare time — patience and clarity go a
long way, in either direction.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing or
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where you are representing
this project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **vibetranslateid@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately. The
maintainer may edit, hide, or delete contributions, and may block accounts, when conduct is
inconsistent with this document — with an explanation whenever it is safe to give one.

Adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately so it actually gets read.
